KEY TAKEAWAYS (5):
- Negative equity means your loan balance is higher than your car's value ("upside down" / "underwater").
- Calculate it: loan payoff amount − car's market value = your negative equity.
- Caused mainly by fast depreciation, long loan terms, small down payments, and rolling old debt into new loans.
- Over 3 in 10 US trade-ins were underwater in early 2026 — average gap ~$7,183.
- Fastest ways out: pay extra toward principal, keep the car longer, or refinance — avoid rolling it into a new loan.
