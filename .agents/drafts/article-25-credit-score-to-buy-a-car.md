## Key Takeaways (6)
1. There's no legal minimum credit score to buy a car — people finance vehicles across the entire 300–850 range.
2. A score of about 661 or higher (prime) usually unlocks a genuinely good interest rate.
3. Below ~600 you can still get approved, but you'll pay a much higher rate and more total interest.
4. Your score mainly moves the interest rate, not whether you're approved — and the rate is where the real money is.
5. Raising your score even one tier before you apply can save you thousands over the loan.
6. A bigger down payment or a co-signer can offset a lower score and get you a better deal.
