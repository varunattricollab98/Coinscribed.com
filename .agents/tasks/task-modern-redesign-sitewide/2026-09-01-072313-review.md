# Modern fintech redesign extended sitewide (v1)

The homepage's "modern fintech" look — elevated cards with gradient icon badges, hover lift, accent glow, `.title-link` headings, and accent CTAs with a nudging arrow — is now applied to the news listing/article/category pages, the calculators index and its shared layout, the bank routing-number index and detail pages plus their two client components, the About page, and the three legal pages. The accent-only palette discipline holds: every touched surface swaps oxblood/ink-primary hovers and the old `bg-white`/`rounded-lg`/`focus:ring-ink-body` patterns for the electric-blue `accent`, and no teal or amber tokens were reintroduced. SEO is untouched — no `metadata`, `generateMetadata`, JSON-LD, `generateStaticParams`, or Open Graph line appears on the removed side of the diff. Calculator math, client-side filtering, and the Sanity data layer are untouched.

Watch for: one CTA on the bank-detail sidebar ("View All Banks") uses `transition-colors hover:gap-2`, so its gap-nudge won't animate and it keys off the link's own hover rather than a `group` — a small divergence from the established CTA pattern (confirmed).

**Verdict**: APPROVED

## High-level view

The redesign is a faithful port of the homepage card system. The calculators index, About coverage grid, and the bank search grid all adopt the reference markup — `group relative … rounded-2xl border-hairline bg-surface … hover:-translate-y-1 hover:border-accent/40`, a blurred accent-glow span, a `bg-accent-gradient` icon badge wrapping a `LineIcon`, and a `group-hover:gap-2` accent CTA — wrapped in `Reveal` with `delay={i*0.05}`. The pages read as one system.

The seven calculators inherit their modern shell from `CalculatorLayout` (accent gradient section markers replacing the oxblood `accent-rule`, elevated `bg-surface` panels). Six calculators additionally swap their result box from `border-oxblood bg-wash` to `border-accent bg-accent-soft`; the retirement calculator is correctly excluded because its box encodes semantic up/down direction with `border-up`/`border-down`, which accent would erase.

The bank area — flagged as the most outdated — got the deepest cleanup: raw `bg-white`/`rounded-lg`/`focus:ring-ink-body` gone, the routing-number table rebuilt on the `.data-table` utility, search inputs on accent focus rings. Client-side filtering in both bank components is unchanged. The legal and About pages got typographic alignment (`.page-title`, `.container-prose`, serif `display` headings, `.link-accent`) rather than card treatment, which is the right call for prose.

Accessibility held or improved: category tabs keep `aria-current`, the new hover-zoom lead image and card lifts carry `motion-reduce:transform-none`, and the newly redesigned cards add that guard where the homepage reference omits it.

<summary>Details</summary>

### Bank-detail sidebar CTA diverges from the pattern

Every modern CTA in this change animates the arrow nudge with `transition-transform … group-hover:gap-2` on a `group` parent. The bank-detail "View All Banks" link instead uses:

```
className="mt-4 inline-flex items-center gap-1 … text-accent transition-colors hover:gap-2 …"
```

`transition-colors` doesn't cover the `gap` change, so the nudge snaps instead of easing, and `hover:gap-2` fires on the link's own hover rather than a card `group`. A cosmetic inconsistency confined to one link — worth aligning with the other CTAs. No motion-reduce concern since gap isn't a transform.

### News lead-image zoom and focus parity

The two listing pages convert the category filter from flat oxblood/ink text links to accent-tinted underline tabs (`border-b-2 border-accent` active, `border-transparent` inactive), preserving `aria-current="page"` on the active tab. The lead story's image gains `rounded-2xl` and a `group-hover:scale-105` hover-zoom guarded by `motion-reduce:transform-none`; the zoom keys off the `<article>` group, but the image `Link` is `aria-hidden`/`tabIndex={-1}`, so a keyboard user focusing the real headline link sees no zoom — flag only if focus parity was intended.

### Calculator result boxes

The six single-line result-box swaps (`border-oxblood bg-wash` → `border-accent bg-accent-soft`, dark-mode `dark:bg-accent/10`) are identical across the pages and don't touch the surrounding `formatCurrency`/results-conditional logic. The retirement calculator keeps `border-up`/`border-down` to signal whether the projection is ahead or behind; accent there would destroy that distinction.
