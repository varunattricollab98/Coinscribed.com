# Coinscribed — Content Roadmap (30 Articles)

**Goal:** Publish 25–30 SEO-optimized evergreen articles in Sanity *before* going
live, so the site launches with depth and starts ranking. US audience.

## Strategy in one line
Target **low-competition, high-intent long-tail** keywords a brand-new site can
actually rank for — not "mortgage calculator" (impossible at first) but "how to
calculate your monthly mortgage payment" (winnable). Every article links to a
relevant calculator or bank page, so content and tools reinforce each other.

## Priority tiers
- **P1 (write first, 12):** directly support existing calculator + bank pages =
  fastest ranking + internal-link power.
- **P2 (write next, 10):** evergreen personal-finance explainers with strong
  featured-snippet potential.
- **P3 (round out, 8):** crypto + timely-but-evergreen pieces for topical breadth.

Difficulty is a rough guess for a NEW site (Easy = winnable in 3–6 months).
Verify actual volume later in Google Keyword Planner / Search Console.

## Article structure (every piece follows this)
1. **H1** = the question/title (contains the keyword naturally)
2. **Intro (2–3 sentences)** — answer the question fast (featured-snippet bait)
3. **H2 sections** — logical steps/points, short paragraphs
4. **A real example with numbers** — tie to a calculator where possible
5. **Internal links** — 2–3 to calculators / bank pages / related articles
6. **FAQ (3–4 Q&As)** at the end
7. **Meta title** (≤60 chars) + **meta description** (≤155 chars) + **slug**

## "Human, not AI-spam" rules
- One specific angle per article; no generic filler.
- Real US numbers/examples; cite sources for any stat.
- Vary sentence length; drop robotic "In conclusion, it is important to note…".
- Every claim must be accurate (YMYL — wrong info kills rankings).

## Publishing cadence
Draft all 30 in Sanity as drafts → review → publish 3–5 per week after launch
(steady publishing signals an active site to Google), OR publish in small
batches. Do NOT dump all 30 on day one and then go silent.
