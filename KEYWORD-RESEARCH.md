## HOW TO USE THIS IN SEMRUSH

1. Paste a keyword into **Keyword Overview** or **Keyword Magic Tool**.
2. Note: **Volume** (US), **KD %**, **Intent**, **CPC**.
3. Screenshot it and send to me. I'll pick the exact target + long-tails and
   write the full article (title, meta, slug, body, FAQ, internal links).

**Priority signal for a NEW site:** favour KD < 35, decent volume (even
100–1,000/mo is fine), informational intent. High CPC is a bonus (means the
topic monetizes well once you add ads/affiliates).

**Best first bets (likely low KD, your pages already support them):**

- routing number vs account number
- apr vs apy
- 401k contribution limits 2026
- what is a stablecoin
- rule of 72
